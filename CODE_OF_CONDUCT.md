# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include being respectful, accepting constructive feedback, showing empathy, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting comments, discrimination, and other inappropriate conduct.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable standards of behavior and taking appropriate corrective action when necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and whenever an individual is officially representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated fairly and appropriately.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will follow appropriate corrective actions depending on the severity of the violation, including warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent removal from the community.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is based on the Contributor Covenant and adapted for this project.
